Source: actual jobs/requests in the B&B inbox (provided 2026-06-13). These are the

## What the demand actually is (ranked by recurrence)
1. **Property management / HOA / vendor work** — the dominant theme. Approved-vendor,
   COI/insurance certificates, work orders, multi-unit, HOA gates.
2. **Door & gate REPAIR** (not just locks) — closers, hinges, latches, patio/sliding
   doors, gate mechanisms. A *repair* intent the site doesn't target as its own thing.
3. **Access control / FOB repair** — "fob reader not working," fix entry system,
   intercoms (incl. schools/classrooms).
4. **Rekey / master key systems** — rekey suites, one-key master systems, fleet keys.
5. **Commercial hardware** — panic bars, Detex exit alarms, door knobs, frames.
6. **Safes** — commercial safe delivery/installation, retail.
7. **New geos beyond current 12 cities** — Vallejo, Morgan Hill, Tiburon.
